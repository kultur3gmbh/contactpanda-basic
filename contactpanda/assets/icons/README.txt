Panda family icon registry

PandaSeasons doors: solid monochrome closed/open door silhouettes, based on the approved outer variants in the upper reference row.

Registry actions: doorClosed and doorOpen.
Usage: data-contactpanda-action="doorClosed" or data-contactpanda-action="doorOpen".
Custom icon classes: contactpanda-action-icon-door-closed and contactpanda-action-icon-door-open.
The same currentColor, dimensions, focus states and action-button system apply as for other family icons.
These entries describe calendar doors. Authentication and license activation icons keep their existing semantics.
